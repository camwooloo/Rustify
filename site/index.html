01 — Architecture

Two processes, one account.

Everything that plays music lives in a daemon. The window is a thin client that connects to it over a local socket, and can be closed, reopened, or replaced without the audio noticing.

the window

rustify

Tauri + WebView. Holds no state of its own. Close it whenever you like.

newline-delimited JSON · 127.0.0.1:4381

the player

rustifyd

librespot. ~15 MB, no GPU context. Its own Spotify Connect device.

02 — What's in it

Built like the app you already know, then further.

Your library, as you expect it

Playlists, albums, artists, Liked Songs, search with filters, a queue, recently played and time-synced lyrics. Playlists are editable, down to dragging rows to reorder them.

Seven layouts

The whole window rearranges — not just its colours. Colour schemes are separate, and Spicetify's are supported.

Extensions

Discord rich presence, a five-band equaliser, a visualiser view, and stats.fm built in. All off until you switch them on.

Miniplayer & fullscreen

A small always-on-top window for while you work, and a full-bleed view for while you don't.

Your listening, in numbers

Connect a stats.fm profile and read your hours, top tracks and top artists without leaving the app.

New in 1.4

Speakers on the network

Idle Connect speakers are found by listening for them rather than by asking Spotify, so they are on the list before anything has woken them — and picking one signs it in.
